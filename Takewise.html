Download Takewise to start singing

This preview can’t use your microphone. Takewise runs as a single file on your computer. Once it’s there you can sing right away, and every take, streak and goal is saved automatically.

  1. Download Takewise.
  2. Open Takewise.html from your Downloads folder in Chrome or Edge.
  3. Allow the microphone when the browser asks.
